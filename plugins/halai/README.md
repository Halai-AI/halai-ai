# Halai for Claude

Create product photos, marketing images, video ads and audio (voice-overs, music and sound effects) from your prompts and reference images, clips and audio, in Arabic or English. Presenter videos support Arabic, English and Spanish.

The plugin connects Claude to your [Halai](https://halaiapp.ai) account. Claude can check your balance, show each job's credit cost before it runs, and display your results in the conversation. Generation spends the credits already in your Halai account, within the spending limits you set for the connection.

## Install in Claude

1. Open **Customize → Plugins** in Claude.
2. Choose **Add → Add marketplace** and enter `Halai-AI/mcp`.
3. Add **Halai**, open its **Connectors** tab, and sign in with your Halai account.

## Install in Claude Code

```
/plugin marketplace add Halai-AI/mcp
/plugin install halai@halai
```

## Try

- "Design an Instagram ad image for my product with Arabic text. Show me the credit cost first."
- "Create a studio product photo from my uploaded image. Show me the credit cost first."
- "Turn my product photo into a short vertical video ad. Show me the credit cost before generating."
